Privacy Policy

How MORPH DESK handles information on Windows and when you choose to use online features.

Your workspace is stored locally

MORPH DESK stores notes, attachments, boards, recordings, preferences and history in your Windows profile. You choose where to export backups. The standalone EXE uninstaller preserves workspace data and optional model downloads. Backups you export remain in the folder you choose.

Local AI and voice

AI and voice tools process data using local models once the required packs are installed. The app accesses your microphone when you start recording or a voice feature.

Optional online services

Weather requests send the selected location to the U.S. National Weather Service. The interactive map requests resources from OpenFreeMap. If you enable online translation, the selected text goes to the configured translation provider when you submit it. Model downloads connect to GitHub or Hugging Face. These providers handle requests under their own policies.

Store and integrations

Microsoft processes Microsoft Store purchases and license information. You choose which content an external client can access through the local Bridge. The client may process the content you share with it.

Manage your data

You can export a backup and remove local content and optional models. Back up any content you wish to keep before deleting files. Use the app’s Model Manager to remove installed model packs. Microsoft manages transactions and account data in the Microsoft Store.
